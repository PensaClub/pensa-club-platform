## 📝 NOTES

- **All fields nullable** - validation handled by schemas
- **String fields for enums** - validated by Zod schemas
- **JSONB for complex data** - flexible structure
- **Strategic indexes** - based on query patterns
- **One-to-one relationships** - for details, location, membership
- **One-to-many relationships** - for members, activities

## 📊 TABLE STRUCTURE

### 📊 TABLE 1: `clubs` (BASIC DATA)
**Migration:** create-club-basic
**Purpose:** Frequently accessed, filtered, and displayed data

**Fields:**
- ✅ id (auto-generated)
- ✅ slug (unique, indexed)
- ✅ name
- ✅ shortDescription (field: 'short_description')
- ✅ foundedYear (field: 'founded_year')
- ✅ status (string - validated by schema)
- ✅ logo
- ✅ mainImage (field: 'main_image')
- ✅ category (string - validated by schema)
- ✅ createdBy (field: 'created_by', FK to user_accounts)
- ✅ isVerified (field: 'is_verified')
- ✅ isPublic (field: 'is_public')
- ✅ rating
- ✅ views
- ✅ followers
- ✅ totalMembers (field: 'total_members', denormalized)
- ✅ createdAt (field: 'created_at')
- ✅ updatedAt (field: 'updated_at')

**Indexes:**
- slug (unique)
- category, status
- created_by
- is_public, status
- rating
- created_at
- total_members

### 📊 TABLE 2: `club_details` (EXTENDED DATA)
**Migration:** create-club-details
**Purpose:** Large/complex data, rarely accessed, admin-heavy

**Fields:**
- ✅ id (auto-generated)
- ✅ clubId (field: 'club_id', FK to clubs)
- ✅ fullDescription (field: 'full_description')
- ✅ gallery (JSONB - array of image URLs)
- ✅ media (JSONB - videos, virtualTour, audioFiles)
- ✅ stats (JSONB - all statistics)
- ✅ management (JSONB - board members)
- ✅ contacts (JSONB - phone, email, socialMedia, workingHours)
- ✅ finances (JSONB - budget, funding, sponsors)
- ✅ regionalInfo (field: 'regional_info', JSONB)
- ✅ achievements (JSONB - awards, certificates)
- ✅ socialImpact (field: 'social_impact', JSONB)
- ✅ pensionersSpecific (field: 'pensioners_specific', JSONB)
- ✅ template (string - indexed)
- ✅ preferences (JSONB - admin settings)
- ✅ createdAt (field: 'created_at')
- ✅ updatedAt (field: 'updated_at')

**Indexes:**
- club_id
- template

### 📊 TABLE 3: `club_locations` (LOCATION DATA)
**Migration:** create-club-location
**Purpose:** Map queries, location filtering

**Fields:**
- ✅ id (auto-generated)
- ✅ clubId (field: 'club_id', FK to clubs)
- ✅ address
- ✅ city
- ✅ municipality
- ✅ region
- ✅ postalCode (field: 'postal_code')
- ✅ coordinates (JSONB - lat, lng)
- ✅ venue (JSONB - type, size, capacity, facilities, accessibility)
- ✅ createdAt (field: 'created_at')
- ✅ updatedAt (field: 'updated_at')

**Indexes:**
- club_id
- city, municipality
- coordinates (GIST index for map queries)

### 📊 TABLE 4: `club_members` (MEMBER DATA)
**Migration:** create-club-members
**Purpose:** Individual club members, will grow large

**Fields:**
- ✅ id (auto-generated)
- ✅ clubId (field: 'club_id', FK to clubs)
- ✅ firstName (field: 'first_name')
- ✅ lastName (field: 'last_name')
- ✅ phone
- ✅ email
- ✅ address
- ✅ photo (JSONB - src, alt)
- ✅ joinDate (field: 'join_date')
- ✅ isActive (field: 'is_active')
- ✅ role (string - validated by schema)
- ✅ createdAt (field: 'created_at')
- ✅ updatedAt (field: 'updated_at')

**Indexes:**
- club_id, is_active
- club_id, role
- email

### 📊 TABLE 5: `club_activities` (ACTIVITY DATA)
**Migration:** create-club-activities
**Purpose:** Events, regular activities, trips, courses

**Fields:**
- ✅ id (auto-generated)
- ✅ clubId (field: 'club_id', FK to clubs)
- ✅ type (string - 'regular', 'event', 'trip', 'course')
- ✅ name
- ✅ description
- ✅ data (JSONB - flexible data per activity type)
- ✅ schedule (JSONB - different structure per type)
- ✅ isActive
- ✅ featured
- ✅ createdAt (field: 'created_at')
- ✅ updatedAt (field: 'updated_at')

**Indexes:**
- club_id
- type
- is_active
- featured

### 📊 TABLE 6: `club_memberships` (MEMBERSHIP RULES)
**Migration:** create-club-membership
**Purpose:** Membership fees, requirements, benefits

**Fields:**
- ✅ id (auto-generated)
- ✅ clubId (field: 'club_id', FK to clubs)
- ✅ totalMembers (field: 'total_members', detailed count)
- ✅ ageGroups (JSONB - age distribution)
- ✅ membershipFee (JSONB - monthly, yearly, currency)
- ✅ requirements (JSONB - membership requirements)
- ✅ benefits (JSONB - membership benefits)
- ✅ createdAt (field: 'created_at')
- ✅ updatedAt (field: 'updated_at')

**Indexes:**
- club_id
